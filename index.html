About Us

Om Dental Clinic is a family dental practice at Balkum Road, Thane West, led by Dr. Darshana Shetty (BDS, Mumbai). We believe good dentistry starts with honesty — we examine carefully, explain clearly, and recommend only what you actually need, never more.
